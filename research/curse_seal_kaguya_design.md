# Curse Seal & Kaguya Bone Techniques — Design Doc

> Naruto Mod (MC 1.20.1 / Forge). Branch: master.  
> Covers: **Curse Seal Stage 1**, **Curse Seal Stage 2**, **Partial Transformation**, **Shikotsumyaku (Kaguya bone bloodline)** and its dances — **Camellia Dance**, **Larch**, **Clematis (Vine / Flower)**, **Seedling Fern**, **Willow**, **Ten-Finger Drilling Bullets**, **Bone Membrane**, **Bone Healing**, plus **Bone Tools**.  
> Goal: curse seal and bone bloodline feel like one family (Orochimaru's mark feeds the Kaguya's body), with distinct risk/reward, visible transformation, and concrete progression gates; no two techniques interchangeable.

---

## 1. Canon grounding

### 1.1 Curse Seal (Juinjutsu)

Orochimaru's forbidden brand: a bite that injects senjutsu chakra, a specially-engineered enzyme, and a fragment of his consciousness. Only ~1 in 10 survive the branding. The mark appears as three identical symbols in a circle; when activated they spread across the body in a pattern unique to the bearer.

- **Cursed Seal of Heaven** (Sasuke, Anko, Sound Four): three tomoe (mitsudomoe), flame-like spread. Favours spiritual energy — speed, stamina, accuracy. In Level 2 Sasuke matches Naruto's one-tail form.
- **Cursed Seal of Earth** (Kimimaro only): three curved rip-like lines. Favours physical energy — strength, defence, HP. In Level 2 Kimimaro becomes dinosaur-like with tail and protruding spine-bones.

**Level 1** — absorbs the bearer's chakra + natural energy, converts to senjutsu chakra, pumps it back. ~2× chakra of higher quality; speed/strength/reflexes up. Drawbacks: constant pain after first manifestation, reacts against the bearer's will when chakra is released, malignant spiritual energy erodes will and stimulates dark emotions, Orochimaru can inflict crippling pain when nearby. The seal invades mind and body until it evolves.

**Level 2** — contamination complete; the enzyme activates, letting the bearer draw natural energy themselves without fatal consequences. Chakra ×10, mutation ("Cursed Seal Transformation"): strength/speed/durability/stamina up, altered physiology, ninjutsu/taijutsu/genjutsu/kekkei genkai pushed past previous limits. Strain is proportionate to power → exhaustion on return. Skilled users perform **partial transformations** (mutate one body part without full change). Artificially inducible via the Mind Awakening Pill (kills the body — shutdown).

**Suppression** — *Fūja Hōin* (Evil Sealing Method, Kakashi): blood-written symbols converge on the mark and chain it. Suppresses auto-activation but is powerless if the bearer *wants* the seal to open. Removed when the seal is removed. Painful; recipients pass out.

**Lore depth**: the seal is a leash, not a gift. Orochimaru's consciousness lives inside it; wearing it too long surrenders the self. "Staying in Level 2 too long loses you forever." The two named seals (Heaven/Earth) recur only in vessels of greatest potential; lesser seals (Sound Four) are weaker and unnamed. Kimimaro's Earth seal is the only one that ever synchronized with a kekkei genkai (Shikotsumyaku) to its full potential.

### 1.2 Shikotsumyaku (Kaguya bone bloodline)

Kekkei genkai of the extinct Kaguya clan; derived from Kaguya Ōtsutsuki's **All-Killing Ash Bones** (a certain-kill: hardened bones fired from skin; on piercing a living target the bone and target deteriorate at molecular level → ash). The clan version is "similar but less lethal."

Three core skills:
1. Control **osteoblasts** (bone production).
2. Control **osteoclasts** (bone breakdown).
3. Regulate **calcium density**.

So long as the body does not tire, these work without limit. Bones can be grown anywhere; usually identical to existing bones, sometimes altered (Camellia = sharpened humerus), sometimes entirely original (Clematis Flower = drill). Density can be raised → cutting power up, resistant to crushing/cutting. Hollow bones possible (flute). Bones can be manipulated as they form, breaking through skin; most often they protrude enough to grab/remove, or stay jutting for offence/defence. Removed bones can be forcefully ejected (Ten-Finger Drilling Bullets). Skin heals rapidly after extraction/retraction. If a removed bone is not duplicated, the body part is misshapen and unusable until replaced.

Shikotsumyaku gives a huge combat advantage: endless bones to suit any circumstance; on the rare occasion an opponent survives, strengthened bones make the body "a fortress." The clan's overestimation of this led them to challenge Kirigakure and be wiped out. Kimimaro was the last survivor; he was terminally ill (possibly hypercalcaemia/cardiac arrest from the bloodline's calcium load). Orochimaru wanted his body; Kabuto could not heal him because his physiology was too different; after death Kabuto replicated the kekkei genkai, and Orochimaru absorbed it from Kabuto.

Kimimaro refers to many techniques as "dances," each named after a plant. The dances mix taijutsu with bone growth.

**Dance set (canon)**:

| Dance | Type | Trigger / requirement | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Camellia (Tsubaki)** | Taijutsu | none (basic) | Modified humerus as a short hiltless bone sword; denser than steel; quick cuts/thrusts. Most basic dance; failed against intoxicated Rock Lee. |
| **Larch (Karamatsu)** | Taijutsu | Curse Seal L1 | Bones protrude all over body (ribs, radius/ulna); defensive "fortress," counters taijutsu; spines damage attackers. Nemesis of pure taijutsu (Rock Lee). |
| **Willow (Yanagi)** | Taijutsu | speed not common for chūnin | Bone spikes from shoulders/elbows/palms/knees; lightning-fast thrusts at precise points. Used vs Naruto. |
| **Clematis: Vine (Tessenka: Tsuru)** | Taijutsu | Curse Seal L2 | Spinal column pulled out as flexible whip-chain; vertebrae sharpened; traps/squeezes. |
| **Clematis: Flower (Tessenka: Hana)** | Taijutsu | follows Vine | Entire forearm encased in a drill-like bone; "hardest bone drill"; pierces shields. |
| **Seedling Fern (Sawarabi)** | Ninjutsu | Curse Seal L2 (ultimate) | Up to ~10,000 bone blades burst from ground; indiscriminate; user can merge with bones and emerge from any (blind-spot finisher). |
| **Ten-Finger Drilling Bullets (Teshi Sendan)** | Taijutsu | none | Distal phalange bones fired from fingertips; spin/drill on impact; excavates skin/flesh/bone; 5 or 10 at a time; nearly limitless rate (regeneration). |
| **Bone Membrane** | defence | none | Hardened bone layer under skin; withstood Gaara's Sand Coffin (which crushes a human). |
| **Bone Healing** | passive | none | Rapid external wound closure after bone extraction; in CS2 healed Gaara's Sand Coffin wounds. |

**Lore depth**: the dances are named after plants whose growth mirrors the technique (fern sprouts from deep earth; camellia = blade-flower; clematis = climbing vine + flower). Kimimaro's illness made him desperate and loyal; his death mid-final-attack is the tragedy. The bloodline's calcium load may itself cause the illness — a genetic disadvantage that makes the power a curse as much as a gift. Kaguya Ōtsutsuki's version (All-Killing Ash Bones) is the "true" form; the clan's is a lesser echo. Black Zetsu used Kaguya's severed arm to fire ash bones.

### 1.3 Clan technique interaction with weapons

- Camellia grows a blade *in place of* the held weapon: the weapon is stowed (or dropped) because the dance is a body-grown blade, not a stance. The blade has 8 charges, no durability bar, no enchantments, NinjaTier.KATANA base (3 damage, -1.9 speed).
- While Camellia is active, **every melee swing** (with any weapon or fist) gains +2 damage and 25% of the total cuts past armour as a second, unblockable magic hit, plus a bleed (Wither, 60 ticks). So the technique scales with the weapon held: a strong sword yields a strong Camellia. This is the "power scaling with weapon."
- Bone Bullets are independent of weapon (channeled, per-shot cost, accelerating rhythm).
- Bone Tools grow a full toolset (pickaxe/axe/shovel/hoe/sword) as items — utility, not combat scaling.
- Larch/Clematis/Willow would be melee-range bone growths that replace or augment the held weapon's reach.

## 2. Progression ladder

```
Basic (everyone) ──► Bone Tools (free) ──► Bone Membrane (defence toggle)
                  └──► Bone Bullets (channeled, Kaguya unlock)
                  └──► Camellia Dance (instant, grows blade) ──► (CS1) Larch (defence counter)
                                                              └──► (CS2) Clematis Vine → Flower
                                                              └──► (CS2) Seedling Fern (ultimate AoE)
                  └──► Willow (rapid thrusts, speed-gated)

Curse Seal One (toggle, health+chakra) ──► Curse Seal Two (requires CS1 + unlock, hunger)
                                      └──► Partial Transformation (mastery of CS2)
                                      └──► Fūja Hōin suppressor (external, Kakashi-style)
```

- **Bone bloodline gating**: Kaguya techniques require the "Kaguya clan" skill node (or scroll). Bone Tools is basic (everyone, lore: anyone can grow bone? No — but mod keeps it basic for QoL). Bone Membrane/Bullets/Camellia require Kaguya node. Larch requires CS1 + Camellia mastery. Clematis/Seedling Fern require CS2 + Camellia mastery. Willow requires speed stat threshold.
- **Curse seal gating**: CS1 is a skill node (or scroll). CS2 requires CS1 learned + CS1 worn long enough (mastery ≥ 25%?). Partial transformation unlocks at CS2 mastery ≥ 50%. Suppressor is an external fūinjutsu (scroll or teacher).
- **Mastery**: per-tick credit while worn (CS1/CS2) or per-use credit (dances). Mastery reduces costs, raises stats, extends durations, slows vessel/bone fatigue.

## 3. Shared resources

### 3.1 Curse seal mark (existing)

- `UNTIL_TAG` (CS1/CS2) refreshed every active tick with `MARK_GRACE = 10` ticks; healing penalty reads it.
- `HEALING_FACTOR`: CS1 = 0.5, CS2 = 0.25 (all healing lands at fraction).
- Upkeep chakra per tick: CS1 0.25, CS2 0.5 (before mastery).
- Open cost: CS1 30 chakra + 4 HP (health damage, not written off bar); CS2 no open cost (reached via CS1 retoggle).
- Hunger: CS2 drains 0.05 exhaustion/tick.
- Boons refreshed every 20 ticks, 40-tick duration: CS1 = Strength I, Speed I, Resistance I; CS2 = Strength II, Speed II, Jump II.
- CS2 adds slow fall (renewed every 10 ticks).
- Visual: CS1 = `ParticleTypes.WITCH` + `EVOKER_CAST_SPELL`; CS2 = `ParticleTypes.SOUL` + `SOUL_ESCAPE`.

### 3.2 Bone fatigue (proposed, new)

Canon: Shikotsumyaku works "so long as the body does not tire." Propose a soft fatigue meter rather than hard limit:

- Each bone grown (Camellia draw, Bone Bullets shot, Larch protrusion, Clematis extraction, Seedling Fern, Willow thrust) adds `FATIGUE` (hidden, per-player persistent data, decays 1/tick).
- At `FATIGUE ≥ 60`: bone density drops (damage −25%, bone blade charges −1). At `≥ 100`: cannot grow new bones until fatigue < 40 (ability refuses with "body too tired" message). Bone Membrane and healing still work.
- Fatigue decay is slower while CS2 is active (body burning hot) — tradeoff: more power, slower recovery.
- Mastery of Kaguya node: fatigue gain −30%, decay +50%.

### 3.3 Bone blade charges (existing)

- `BoneBladeItem`: 8 charges, no durability, no enchant, NinjaTier.KATANA (3 dmg, -1.9 speed). Last charge breaks blade with grey message. Re-drawing Camellia with blade in hand renews charges instead of stacking.

## 5. Power scaling with weapon (summary)

| Technique | Scales with weapon? | How |
|---|---|---|
| Camellia Dance | **Yes (primary)** | Bonus +2/+3 added to swing amount; 25%/35% armour bypass as second hit; bleed. Stronger sword → stronger cut. CS2 active ×1.5. |
| Bone Bullets | No (independent) | Optional: +weapon attack ×0.25. |
| Willow | Partial | Thrust damage + Camellia bonus if active. |
| Clematis Flower | No | Fixed 12/16 (drill). |
| Seedling Fern | No | Fixed 8/11 (AoE). |
| Bone Tools sword | Yes (utility) | Damage scales with Kaguya mastery. |
| Larch counter | No | Fixed 2/3. |
| Curse Seal boons | Yes (indirect) | Strength I/II multiplies weapon damage. |

Design principle: **bone techniques that replace the weapon (Camellia blade, Willow thrusts) scale with the wielder's strength/weapon; bone techniques that are projectiles or AoE (Bullets, Clematis, Fern) are fixed and paid per use.** This keeps the Kaguya player choosing between "augment my sword" and "spend chakra on guaranteed damage."

## 7. Tradeoffs summary

| Technique | Power | Cost | Risk | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Curse Seal 1 | mid (stats +20%) | 30 chakra + 4 HP + upkeep | healing halved, will erosion | sustained fights |
| Curse Seal 2 | high (stats +40%, jump) | upkeep + hunger | healing quartered, madness risk, exhaustion | boss fights, finishing |

## 9. Open questions / future work

- **CS2 visual transformation**: should the mod render grey skin/white hair/hand-wings? Requires a client layer (like ChakraCloakLayer). Proposed: optional, gated by config; default particles-only to match current style.
- **Seedling Fern block placement**: should bone blades be placed as temporary blocks (like earth wall)? Could obstruct movement and add terrain. Proposed: particles-only first; optional config `boneForestPlacesBlocks`.
- **Bone Bullets weapon scaling**: should shard damage scale with held weapon? Could blur "suppression vs duel" choice. Proposed: keep fixed; revisit if playtesting shows bullets too weak.
- **Fatigue visibility**: should fatigue be a HUD bar? Proposed: hidden by default, shown as "tired" message at ≥60; optional HUD in settings.
- **Partial transformation as separate ability vs CS2 sub-mode**: sub-mode keeps combo space; separate ability is clearer in menu. Proposed: separate ability gated by CS2 mastery.
- **Suppressor lore**: in canon only Kakashi can cast; in mod, make it a scroll-learned fūinjutsu available to all (balance). Alternative: gate behind a teacher NPC.
- **Kaguya clan gating**: should bone techniques require a clan selection system (not yet in mod)? Proposed: skill-tree node "Kaguya Bloodline" for now; clan system later.
- **Bone Membrane + Larch stacking**: allow both (defence + counter) or make mutually exclusive? Proposed: allow stacking; fatigue cost doubles.
- **Camellia + CS2 interaction**: proposed ×1.5 bonus and +4 charges; needs tuning.

## 11. Lore depth (for wiki / item tooltips)

- **Curse Seal**: Orochimaru's leash disguised as power. "Only one in ten survive." The mark is a fragment of Orochimaru's mind; wearing it erodes the self. Heaven favours spirit, Earth favours body; Kimimaro's Earth is the only one that ever synchronized with a kekkei genkai. Staying in Level 2 too long loses the self forever. The suppressor chains the mark but cannot remove it — and is powerless against a willing bearer.
- **Shikotsumyaku**: the clan's fatal pride. "Bodies like fortresses" led them to challenge Kirigakure and be annihilated. The bloodline's calcium load may itself be the illness that killed Kimimaro. Kimimaro's loyalty to Orochimaru was the one thing he had; his death mid-final-strike is the tragedy. The dances are named after plants — growth, beauty, and burial.
- **Kaguya Ōtsutsuki**: the origin. All-Killing Ash Bones disintegrate at the molecular level — a certain-kill. The clan's Shikotsumyaku is a lesser echo. Black Zetsu fired ash bones from Kaguya's severed arm.
- **Camellia**: the simplest dance, and the one that failed against a drunk Lee — a reminder that technique is not invincibility. The bone blade has 8 cuts and then breaks; it is not a sword to keep.
- **Bone Bullets**: "the Kaguya do not throw things, they grow them." Suppression, not duelling. The rhythm accelerates as the hand learns; a master keeps the line open.
- **Seedling Fern**: the last resort. Ten thousand blades, indiscriminate, and the caster becomes one of them. Kimimaro died before he could finish.
